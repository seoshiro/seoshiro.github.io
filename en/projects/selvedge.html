SELVEDGE.

One graphic.
A whole collection.

An apparel workroom for artwork placement, colorways, revision comparison, and a clear visual handoff.

Open demoSource code

MY ROLE

I shape the workflow and visual direction, guide implementation with AI and review the result. SELVEDGE focuses on a consistent collection, named revisions and a clear handoff. AI-assisted implementation and verification are documented separately.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Canvas
  • IndexedDB

Who it helps

For small apparel brands and merch designers: arrange one graphic across tee colorways and prepare a clear visual conversation with a printer.

01

Starting point

One piece of artwork and several choices about garment color, placement, and scale.

02

What you leave with

A coordinated collection, named revisions, a PNG overview, a PDF visual proof, and an editable project file.

03 / Under the surface

Under the surface.

  1. 01

    An illustrated garment and print measurements keep placement legible without promising photorealistic 3D. Physical size and color still need printer confirmation.

  2. 02

    Shared placement across the collection with per-colorway edits avoids repeating the same action six times while keeping individual variants editable.

  3. 03

    Named revisions and a portable project preserve an editable direction, instead of keeping only a final PNG. PNG and PDF are visual proofs.

A documented correction

Valid JPEG/WebP uploads could exceed the stored PNG limit and receive an inaccurate “2 MB” error. Adaptive resizing now offers a review with final dimensions and DPI. Cancellation preserves the current project; regressions check transparency and actual exports.
