AYQYN.

Observe with context.
Leave decisions to people.

A Windows local proctoring prototype: participant consent, camera preparation, on-device observations and a separate human review of events.

Open demoSource code

MY ROLE

AI-assisted product creation: I define the brief, choose and connect tools, direct AI-assisted implementation, and review the visual result. The repository documents the implementation and checks.

  • Electron
  • JavaScript
  • ONNX Runtime
  • MediaPipe
01

Starting point

Prepare a local session with participant consent and camera calibration. A test scenario and browser replay let you explore the workflow without a camera.

02

What you leave with

An observation timeline, reviewer decisions with reasons, and a JSON report without images.

03 / Under the surface

Under the surface.

  1. 01

    YOLO and MediaPipe process frames on the device in a separate process. Processing and snapshot consent are separate; saving snapshots is off by default.

  2. 02

    The reviewer sees duration, face count, frame quality and model confidence for phone detection. They confirm or dismiss the observation and explain their decision.

  3. 03

    JSON reports contain no images. The public browser replay shows saved detector results as diagrams without running a camera; photographs are used in the local test replay.

04 / Practical boundaries

Practical boundaries

A prototype for Qostanai AI Industry Hackathon 2026, Case 3. Gaze direction is approximate; observations do not prove misconduct and require human review. This is not a certified examination environment. Browser replay does not test camera accuracy.
