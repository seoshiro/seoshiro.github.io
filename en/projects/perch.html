PERCH.

A room.
A few possibilities.

A local-first furniture planner with linked 2D and 3D views. Shape the room, arrange 12 parametric furniture types, and compare layouts before moving anything.

Open demoSource code

MY ROLE

I define the brief, direct AI-assisted implementation and review the result. For PERCH, the focus is a coherent path through the plan, 3D view and layout comparison. Code and automated checks were prepared with AI; the repository records their results and limits.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Three.js
  • SVG

Who it helps

For someone comparing layouts before moving their furniture. Real room measurements are the starting point, rather than an image without scale.

01

Starting point

A rectangular room, its measurements and openings, and furniture you want to make space for.

02

What you leave with

Editable layout alternatives, fit notes, a portable JSON project, a dimensioned SVG plan, a 3D PNG, and a printable room report.

03 / Under the surface

Under the surface.

  1. 01

    Move furniture in the plan; inspect it in 3D. Separating these actions avoids a conflict with camera orbit. Numeric fields provide a precise alternative to dragging.

  2. 02

    One room model drives both views. Resizing a sofa changes its plan and volume together, without maintaining separate versions of the layout.

  3. 03

    Local saves and portable JSON let you start without an account and compare independent room copies. The tradeoff is no cloud sync; export a backup to move devices.

A documented correction

The release audit found React effect cleanup cancelling plan drags too early. The correction was checked with completed and cancelled gestures, repeated numeric moves, and undo/redo. This is a documented implementation correction, not a user-research finding.

Try a complete workflow

Open a furnished room → change its dimensions → move the sofa in the plan → duplicate and compare two layouts → export JSON or a dimensioned SVG.

04 / Practical boundaries

Practical boundaries

A furnishing study for rectangular rooms, not a construction or safety document. Materials and light are illustrative; fit notes do not certify walking clearance. No cloud sync or collaboration. Export JSON to move work between devices.
