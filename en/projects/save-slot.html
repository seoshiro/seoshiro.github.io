SAVE SLOT.

Six worlds.
One playable shelf.

A retro game shelf that opens into six playable worlds. Browse 3D boxes, insert a cartridge into the console, then play a complete Canvas2D mini-game.

Open demoSource code

MY ROLE

I define the concept, visual direction and interaction brief, guide AI-assisted implementation and review the result. The code and automated checks were prepared with AI. The console began as an AI-generated model and was cleaned in Blender; I do not claim hand-modeling or unaided coding.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Three.js
  • GSAP
  • Canvas2D

Who it helps

For someone who wants to explore a small interactive world, rather than stop at a product animation. The physical box-to-console ritual leads to a game that can actually be completed.

01

Starting point

Choose a world by dragging, with the arrows or the six selection buttons. Open its box, insert the cartridge and play with keyboard or touch controls. The interface supports English and Russian.

02

What you leave with

Six distinct mini-games with objectives, hazards and a finish, a reversible shelf-to-console sequence, an enlarged game view, pause and confirmed restart.

03 / Under the surface

Under the surface.

  1. 01

    One persistent Three.js scene keeps the shelf, opening boxes and cartridge insertion connected. GSAP drives the reversible sequence. The procedural box geometry uses supplied artwork; the console is an AI-generated asset cleaned in Blender.

  2. 02

    Canvas2D powers all six games and feeds the console screen. A larger flat view and a playable light fallback keep gameplay available without WebGL.

  3. 03

    Each cartridge keeps independent progress in the current tab/session. A same-tab reload can restore it; blocked storage falls back to page memory. It is not a permanent or cloud save.

A documented correction

The six-game audit found that blocked storage lost cartridge progress on switching, long-running reverse traffic left the DUSK route, and a second touch did not reliably activate MOSS. Page-scoped snapshots, positive cyclic traffic wrapping and pointer-press actions were added and checked separately. The repository records full playthroughs and the limits of emulated touch.

Try a complete workflow

Choose MOSS SIGNAL → open the box → insert the cartridge → restore three beacons → reach the portal → return to the shelf. Try the enlarged screen, pause or a same-tab reload along the way.

04 / Practical boundaries

Practical boundaries

Independent AI-assisted portfolio concept, not a commercial release or client project. Six complete small games, not a full game platform. Progress belongs to the current browser tab/session and is not guaranteed after closing it. The app supports RU/EN; this case also has a Kazakh translation. Checks use Chrome and emulated touch; physical phones, Safari and Firefox remain untested.
