A dark headphone design study with original procedural 3D geometry, a natural camera tour and a working visual-card configurator.

Live projectSource code

PROJECT CONTEXT

Independent portfolio project. Implementation, design decisions, and verification are documented in the source repository.

  • JavaScript
  • Three.js
  • Procedural 3D
  • PNG export
01

Starting point

Explore the object in English or Russian. Choose Graphite, Titanium or Oxide, follow the camera tour or pin a detail view.

02

What you leave with

A responsive product concept and a PNG card with the selected headphone render, finish, background and localized notes, without ordering or payment.

03 / Under the surface

Under the surface.

  1. 01

    One original procedural assembly creates all five views: metal frame, visible adjustment and pivots, oval cushions, stitched seams and surface-anchored droplets. No third-party headphone mesh or product photography is used.

  2. 02

    Real WebGL geometry and its optimized still renders share the same materials and lighting. Desktop scroll and subtle pointer parallax reveal the object; manual views remain pinned until the user resumes following scroll.

  3. 03

    English and Russian keep a stable product stage. Bounded loading, persisted motion-off, reduced motion, keyboard dialogs and color-aware stills preserve access without WebGL. The configurator exports a real visual PNG card.

04 / Practical boundaries

Practical boundaries

A fictional design and material study. No manufactured product, acoustic measurement, tested comfort or water-resistance claim. Chrome desktop and emulated mobile were checked; physical Safari devices were not.
