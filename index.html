A BIT ABOUT MY APPROACH

The details are
the work.

I’m Beibars, also known as seoshiro. My projects sit between expressive interfaces and practical browser tools. I like taking something scattered—references, revisions, files—and giving it a clear shape.

TypeScript / React / Browser APIs
  1. 01

    Make the next step clear.

    A good interface helps you understand what you have, what changed, and what to do next.

  2. 02

    Treat data with care.

    Visible saves, bounded imports, and portable backups make a small tool easier to trust.

  3. 03

    Make it feel considered.

    Typography, motion, and keyboard behavior belong in the same conversation as the code.
